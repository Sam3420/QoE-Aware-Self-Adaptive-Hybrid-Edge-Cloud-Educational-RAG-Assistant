# Concept ids for labelling (evaluation/tagging/questions.csv)

Write one id per question in `gold_concept_id`, or `none` if the question is off-topic.

## control_and_coordination (draft)
- `sensory_receptors`: Sensory Receptors. Learners should identify where and how organisms detect environmental stimuli.
- `neuron_structure`: Neuron Structure. Learners should know the three main parts of a neuron and their functions.
- `synaptic_transmission`: Synaptic Transmission. Learners should understand how an electrical impulse triggers chemical release across a synapse.
- `reflex_arc`: Reflex Arc. Learners should describe the pathway that produces a rapid involuntary response.
- `brain_regions`: Brain Regions. Learners should identify the three major parts of the brain.
- `cerebellum_function`: Cerebellum Function. Learners should explain the role of the cerebellum in coordinating voluntary movements and balance.
- `plant_tropisms`: Plant Tropisms. Learners should recognize different growth‑directed movements of plants toward stimuli.
- `auxin_mechanism`: Auxin Mechanism. Learners should understand how auxin distribution causes phototropic bending.
- `animal_hormones`: Animal Hormones. Learners should define hormones as chemical messengers released by endocrine glands into the blood.
- `hormonal_feedback`: Hormonal Feedback. Learners should describe how hormone release is regulated by feedback mechanisms.
- `muscle_contraction`: Muscle Contraction. Learners should explain how nervous impulses cause muscle fibers to shorten.

## heredity (draft)
- `asexual_reproduction`: Asexual Reproduction. Learners should understand that asexual reproduction produces offspring with very few differences due to minor DNA copying errors.
- `sexual_reproduction`: Sexual Reproduction. Learners should know that sexual reproduction creates greater genetic diversity because offspring receive DNA from two parents.
- `genetic_variation`: Genetic Variation. Learners should recognize that variation among individuals increases a species' chances of survival in changing environments.
- `dominant_recessive`: Dominant & Recessive Traits. Learners should be able to identify which trait is expressed (dominant) and which is hidden (recessive) in a heterozygote.
- `monohybrid_ratio`: Monohybrid Ratio. Learners should calculate the 3:1 phenotypic ratio in the F2 generation of a monohybrid cross.
- `independent_assortment`: Independent Assortment. Learners should understand that different gene pairs segregate independently during gamete formation, producing new trait combinations.
- `dihybrid_ratio`: Dihybrid Ratio. Learners should predict the 9:3:3:1 phenotypic ratio resulting from a dihybrid cross.
- `gene_definition`: Gene Definition. Learners should know that a gene is a DNA segment that provides the information for making a specific protein.
- `sex_determination`: Sex Determination. Learners should explain how sex chromosomes (XX or XY) determine the sex of human offspring.
- `chromosome_number_restoration`: Chromosome Number Restoration. Learners should understand that fertilisation restores the normal diploid chromosome number by combining one set from each parent.

## how_do_organisms_reproduce (draft)
- `dna_replication_basics`: DNA replication. Learners should understand that reproduction involves making a copy of DNA, which is not perfectly accurate and leads to variation.
- `asexual_fission`: Binary fission. Learners should know that unicellular organisms reproduce by splitting into two daughter cells.
- `multiple_fission`: Multiple fission. Learners should recognize that some parasites produce many daughter cells simultaneously.
- `fragmentation_reproduction`: Fragmentation. Learners should understand that multicellular filaments can break into pieces, each developing into a new individual.
- `regeneration_and_budding`: Regeneration & budding. Learners should be able to differentiate regeneration (whole organism from a piece) from budding (outgrowth that detaches).
- `vegetative_propagation`: Vegetative propagation. Learners should know that many plants reproduce asexually from roots, stems, or leaves, producing genetically similar offspring.
- `spore_formation_and_protection`: Spore protection. Learners should understand that spores are enclosed in thick walls that safeguard them until favorable conditions arise.
- `flower_anatomy_pistil`: Pistil anatomy. Learners should identify the three parts of the pistil: ovary, style, and stigma.
- `pollination_and_fertilisation`: Pollination & fertilisation. Learners should distinguish self‑pollination from cross‑pollination and describe the pollen tube pathway leading to fertilisation.
- `human_puberty_and_reproductive_systems`: Human puberty & reproduction. Learners should recognize the physiological changes at puberty and the basic structure of male and female reproductive systems.
- `menstruation_cycle`: Menstruation cycle. Learners should know the monthly cycle of egg release, possible fertilisation, and shedding of the uterine lining if fertilisation does not occur.
- `reproductive_health_methods`: Reproductive health methods. Learners should identify various contraceptive and disease‑prevention methods, including barriers, hormonal, intra‑uterine, and surgical options.

## life_processes (approved)
- `life_processes_overview`: Life Processes. Learners should understand the four major life processes and their purpose in organisms.
- `autotrophic_nutrition_photosynthesis`: Autotrophic Nutrition. Learners should know how autotrophs make food via photosynthesis and its overall equation.
- `heterotrophic_nutrition_types`: Heterotrophic Nutrition. Learners should differentiate holozoic, saprophytic, and parasitic modes of nutrition.
- `human_digestion_process`: Human Digestion. Learners should describe the sequence of digestive organs, enzymes, and the role of peristalsis.
- `respiration_aerobic_vs_anaerobic`: Respiration Types. Learners should compare aerobic respiration with anaerobic fermentation and their products.
- `respiratory_structure_mechanics`: Human Respiratory System. Learners should understand the pathway of air, the role of alveoli, and the mechanics of breathing.
- `human_circulatory_system`: Human Circulation. Learners should explain double circulation, heart chambers, and the functions of arteries, veins, and capillaries.
- `plant_transport_xylem_phloem`: Plant Transport. Learners should describe xylem water movement and phloem translocation of photosynthates.
- `kidney_filtration`: Kidney Filtration. Learners should know the structure of a nephron and the role of Bowman's capsule and glomerulus in filtration.
- `stomatal_control`: Stomatal Control. Learners should understand how guard cells regulate opening and closing of stomata.

## our_environment (draft)
- `ecosystem_definition`: Ecosystem Definition. Learner should understand what constitutes an ecosystem, including biotic and abiotic components.
- `producers`: Producers. Learner should know which organisms produce their own food and the term used for them.
- `consumers`: Consumers. Learner should recognize organisms that obtain food from producers and their categories.
- `decomposers`: Decomposers. Learner should understand the role of microorganisms in breaking down dead material.
- `trophic_levels`: Trophic Levels. Learner should identify the hierarchical levels in a food chain.
- `ten_percent_law`: Ten Percent Law. Learner should know the approximate energy transfer efficiency between trophic levels.
- `food_web`: Food Web. Learner should understand that feeding relationships form an interconnected network.
- `biological_magnification`: Biological Magnification. Learner should grasp how non‑degradable chemicals accumulate up the food chain.
- `ozone_layer`: Ozone Layer. Learner should know the protective role of stratospheric ozone against UV radiation.
- `biodegradable_waste`: Biodegradable Waste. Learner should identify materials that can be broken down by biological processes.
- `non_biodegradable_waste`: Non‑Biodegradable Waste. Learner should recognize materials that persist in the environment and do not decompose biologically.
